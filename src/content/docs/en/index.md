Apertrust is a free, non-destructive raw photo editor. This guide shows you how to get the photo you want from it.

- **[Getting started](getting-started/first-edit/):** your first photo, from opening the raw to exporting it.
- **How-to's:** what you want to do, step by step, such as making a photo brighter.
- **Tools:** each tool, what it is for and what each of its controls does. A tool's Help, at the bottom of its ⋯ menu, opens its page here.
- **Concepts:** why Apertrust works the way it does, when you want to know.
